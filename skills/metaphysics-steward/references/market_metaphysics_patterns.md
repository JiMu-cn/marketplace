# Market Metaphysics Patterns

Traditional divination methods can be mapped to market dynamics for "Laicai" style reporting.

## 1. 損上益下 (Decrease Top, Increase Bottom)
- **Origin**: Based on the *Yi Jing* logic of the *Yi* (Increase) and *Sun* (Decrease) hexagrams.
- **Market Mapping**: Asset Rotation.
- **Pattern**: When a high-position leader (Top/SMIC) shows signs of exhaustion (Sun), and a low-position potential leader (Bottom/Aerospace) shows a catalyst (Yi), the steward "decreases the top" to "increase the bottom."
- **Usage**: Used to justify taking profits on winning positions to fund new entries.

## 2. 庚子金水 (Geng-Zi Metal/Water)
- **Pattern**: Strong Metal-Water flow.
- **Market Mapping**: High liquidity and volatility.
- **Action**: Aggressive trading. In such environments, "water" (money) moves quickly. The steward should prioritize speed and rapid execution (Rotation Atomicity).

## 3. 風雷益 (Wind and Thunder: Increase)
- **Pattern**: A combination of the Sun (Wind) and Zhen (Thunder) trigrams.
- **Market Mapping**: Momentum breakout.
- **Interpretation**: Thunder represents the "spark" (news/catalyst), and Wind represents the "spread." This indicates a fast-moving trend where chasing (Limit Buy at current) is favorable.

## 4. 泰极而临 (Tai Peak to Lin Approach)
- **Pattern**: Transition from Tai (泰, Peace/Peak) to Lin (临, Approaching/Supervision).
- **Market Mapping**: Consolidation after a significant rally.
- **Action**: "Supervision" (Lin). Do not add to winning positions that are retreating; instead, monitor support levels ("Overseeing"). This is a defensive stance within an aggressive campaign.
- **Case Study**: 2026-05-29 AI Infra pullback.

## 5. 坤变师 (Kun to Shi: Broad Stability to Strategic Deployment)
- **Pattern**: Transition from Kun (坤, Earth/Receptive) to Shi (师, Army/Discipline).
- **Market Mapping**: Moving from a phase of passive holding/accumulation to active, disciplined rotation.
- **Action**: Pruning weak assets (e.g. lagging data stocks) to concentrate force in sector leaders (e.g. CPO core).
